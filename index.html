The Classic Setup

Carrom is a tabletop game of Indian origin. A standard set uses black and white carrom men, one red queen and a larger striker. The striker is used to hit the carrom men toward the corner pockets.


NQ Royale keeps the real-board look while adding a custom website game rule that locks the striker to the active player's baseline.
